Container loading: plywood weighs out before it cubes out

Plywood is dense cargo. A container of packing plywood reaches its permitted payload well before the space is full, so the real question is never "how many sheets fit" — it is how many sheets the box, the shipping line and the destination's road rules will let you carry. Sheets per container is a weight calculation: thickness, panel density, pallet weight and the road limit at the discharge end.

We work both box sizes. A 20ft suits smaller orders and weight-restricted lanes; a 40ft carries more sheets only where the lane's road rules allow the payload, since the cap is on weight, not volume. Loose-loaded and palletised counts differ, and we quote both where it matters. Every export quote carries a loading plan — sheets per container, net and gross weight — so you are not surprised at a weighbridge and you are not paying ocean freight to ship air.

ISPM-15: what actually applies to plywood

This is the most common point of confusion in plywood export from India, so here it is plainly. Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood material, manufactured under heat and adhesive pressure, so the panels themselves need no stamp and no fumigation at any border that follows the standard.

What ISPM-15 does cover is solid-wood packaging — the sawn-timber runners and battens inside boxes, crates and pallets. Our box and crate process is ISPM-15 compliant, so packaging that includes solid-timber components ships with treatment already handled. If you are weighing treatment routes for your own packaging, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide covers the difference.

FAQ

Does plywood need ISPM-15 treatment for export?

No. Plywood is a processed wood material and is exempt from ISPM-15, so the panels ship without a stamp or fumigation. The standard applies to solid-wood packaging such as timber runners in boxes, crates and pallets — and our packaging process is ISPM-15 compliant, so those components ship with treatment handled.

50% advance with the balance before dispatch for standard products; custom specifications are 100% advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T). Export quotes are in USD, FOB Cochin by default.

How many plywood sheets fit in a 20ft or 40ft container?

It depends on weight, not volume. Plywood reaches a container's permitted payload before the space fills, and destination road limits can lower the cap further, so the count varies with thickness, density and lane. Every quote includes a loading plan with sheets per container and net and gross weights.
