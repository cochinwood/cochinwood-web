Why Perumbavoor sets the pace

Perumbavoor, in Ernakulam district, is Kerala’s plywood capital. Several hundred veneer and plywood units operate in and around the town. Raw material is close: rubberwood comes off plantation estates across central Kerala as trees finish their latex life and are felled for replanting. The port is close too — Cochin Port sits about 40 km away. Captive plantation timber, dense peeling and pressing capacity, and a container berth within the hour: that is why Perumbavoor is the reference market for packing-grade plywood in South India.

Desk commentary: what is driving the market

Qualitative observations from our desk this quarter. We publish drivers, not forecasts, and we do not dress observations up as numbers we cannot verify.

Rubberwood log supply. Log availability in the cluster follows plantation replanting cycles, and felling decisions follow latex economics, not plywood demand. Our desk sees log flow as the swing factor mills watch most closely; when estate clearances bunch up or thin out, peeling schedules move with them.

Core-veneer availability. The cluster runs on locally peeled rubberwood core veneer, with eucalyptus in some builds. Dried core stock sets the working floor under panel prices — pressing capacity is rarely the constraint; dry veneer is.

Packing demand and the container cycle. Orders indicate steady case and crate work from engineering exporters shipping through Cochin Port. Export crating tracks the container cycle: when sailings and box availability hold steady, crate and pallet orders follow. Our export desk reads the same signal in Gulf-bound enquiries.

Demand signals: which panels are moving

From our order book this quarter:

  • 12mm and 18mm MR packing dominate case work. Heavy-machinery crates typically specify one or both — 12mm for panels and lids, 18mm for bases and load-bearing faces.
  • 8mm holds the lighter end: boxes, partitions and pallet decks.
  • Okoume-faced panels lead export enquiries — lower sheet weight and a cleaner face for shipments to the UAE and Israel. Detail on the Okoume plywood page.
