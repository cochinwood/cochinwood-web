Perumbavoor is India's plywood belt

Most Indian plywood buyers know the name. Perumbavoor, on the eastern edge of the Kochi metropolitan area, carries one of the densest concentrations of plywood and veneer production in the country — peeling units, core yards, resin suppliers, timber markets and seasoned operators within a few kilometres of each other. When a panel needs a specific core build or an odd size, the belt can produce it without long waits.

That ecosystem is our home ground. Our group has manufactured plywood here since 1986 — four decades of buying veneer, grading cores and loading trucks in the same market. Cochin Wood Industries puts that experience behind every order: the same panel, the same grade, the same dimensions, shipment after shipment.

Kerala timber, Kerala cores

The belt exists because the raw material does. Kerala's rubber plantations replant on a fixed cycle, which puts a steady stream of rubberwood into the Perumbavoor timber market, and eucalyptus is peeled for core veneer close by. Buying core timber at source, rather than freighting it across the country, is what lets a Kerala plywood manufacturer hold consistent core quality at packing-grade cost. The species we work with, and where each fits, are documented at woods we use.

Pan-India dispatch, and a port 40 km away

For Indian buyers we dispatch from Kochi to any state. Material is priced ex-factory with GST and transport extra, and we plan truckloads against your unloading point.
