Why serious buyers quote per spec, not per rate card

Plywood is not a commodity with one market rate. Timber, veneer and resin are bought fresh every month, and their costs move. A rate card printed in January is wrong by March, so any seller still honouring it has either padded the margin to cover the drift or will quietly substitute a lighter panel. Procurement teams that buy plywood in volume know this, which is why they send a specification and ask for a dated, written quote instead of asking for a rate list.

We work the same way. As a plywood manufacturer in India whose group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986, we price each enquiry against current input costs, and the quote we send is a number we will load against.

Grade is the biggest step: IS 303 vs IS 710

Moisture-resistant (MR) plywood under IS 303 is the workhorse for packing cases, crates and pallets. It is built and priced for one hard journey, not twenty years of service, and our packing plywood sits here. BWR (boiling water resistant) sheets move up the ladder for humid or repeated-wetting duty. At the top sits marine plywood to IS 710, with boiling-waterproof bonding and denser construction, priced accordingly. Paying marine rates for a one-way export crate wastes money; putting packing ply where IS 710 belongs costs far more when it fails. Match the grade to the duty and the price follows.

Face veneer and core: where identical-looking sheets diverge

Two sheets of the same thickness and grade can still be priced apart because of what they are made from. A commercial face is the baseline. Okoume and Gurjan are premium face veneers, offered as options on MR-packing and BWR constructions rather than as separate grades, so an Okoume-faced sheet is quoted above its commercial-faced twin. Calibration is the final step up. A calibrated E1 panel with an Okoume face on a eucalyptus core, a 16mm sheet at about 32kg, is furniture-line material held to tight thickness tolerance and priced well above packing-tolerance ply.

Ex-factory vs delivered, and where GST sits

The quoted rate is only half the invoice. Within India, plywood is normally quoted ex-factory, with GST and transport extra, and we state that basis on every quote we issue. When comparing suppliers, confirm three things in writing: whether the rate is ex-factory or delivered, whether GST is included or extra, and what quantity the transport assumes. A rate that looks lower on the phone often stops looking lower once the basis is on paper. Export buyers are quoted FOB Cochin in USD, with every element of the basis stated the same way.

What is the plywood price per sheet in India?

There is no single figure. A sheet of the same size spans a wide range between 6mm MR packing grade and 18mm IS 710 marine, and face veneer, core species, calibration and order volume move it further. What determines the price of plywood?

The grade (MR, BWR or BWP), the core, the face (Okoume or Gurjan), the thickness, the sheet size, your order volume and the delivery distance. Change any one and the number moves — which is why we quote against your exact specification.

Does thickness change the plywood price?
