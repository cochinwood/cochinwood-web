How plywood is made at our unit

The line runs end-to-end: log peeling, veneer drying, glue spreading, lay-up, hot pressing, then sizing, sanding and grading. Bond is matched to the job — MR for interior, BWR and BWP/marine for damp and export service — with E1 low-emission glue available on request.

In-process quality control covers moisture content, glue-line and calibration, so panels arrive flat and consistent. Every grade ships with a material guarantee and a technical data sheet. The unit is geared for full-truckload and repeat container orders rather than one-off retail sheets.
