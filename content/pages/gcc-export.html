Plywood Export to the GCC

These posts cover how we ship plywood from Kochi to the Gulf — into Dammam, Riyadh, Khobar, Doha, Muscat and Sharjah. Each one gets into what matters for a sea-freight order: packing that survives the container, FOB and CIF-ready documentation, and loading planned against destination road weight caps.

Cochin Wood is a Perumbavoor plywood manufacturer backed by group operations running since 1986. We build export crates and pallets with ISPM-15 treated timber, load Okoume and packing-grade sheets to fit South-India ports, and quote FOB against your terms.
