How the quality holds

Grades are made to Indian Standards: IS 303 covers the MR and BWR packing and commercial grades, and IS 710 covers BWP marine plywood. Panels are measured before loading, thickness and dimensions checked against the order, and every shipment carries our material guarantee: what arrives matches the spec sheet you quoted from.

One detail that saves exporters paperwork: plywood panels themselves are exempt from ISPM-15 treatment and marking, because plywood is processed wood. Where assembled boxes and crates need marking, we build under an ISPM-15 compliant process. The two treatment routes are compared in heat treatment vs methyl bromide.

Is plywood exempt from ISPM-15?

Yes. Plywood panels are processed wood and are exempt from ISPM-15 treatment and marking. Assembled plywood boxes and crates that require marking are built under an ISPM-15 compliant process.
