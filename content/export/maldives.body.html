A short lane, and why that matters more here than elsewhere

Almost everything a construction or fit-out job in the Maldives consumes arrives by sea. That makes the length of the lane a real commercial variable rather than a detail: on a short one you can order against a project’s actual progress instead of against a forecast made months earlier, hold less material on site, and re-order without the schedule absorbing the delay. Our factories sit about 40 km from Cochin Port, so a container that finishes loading in the morning can gate in at the terminal the same day.

The other half of the argument is water. Panels bound for island work face humidity in transit, humidity in storage and, often, humidity in service. The bond you specify is worth more attention here than on a dry inland job, and it is the first thing we will ask you about.

Our group has pressed plywood at Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. Quotes are in USD, FOB Cochin by default, and come back within one business day.

A separate fortnightly Government of India service anchored at Tuticorin carries mostly bulk cargo and is not a standard FCL booking option.

Getting the grade right the first time

The mistake that costs money on island work is specifying by price rather than by exposure. MR-bonded packing plywood is a dry-service, one-trip material; it is the wrong panel for anything that will meet standing water, and no amount of coating fixes a glue line chosen for the wrong duty. BWR handles moisture in service. IS 710 BWP is the boil-proof grade, and it is what we would put into boat work, jetty structures and any wet area that has to last.

Each grade ships with its technical data sheet, and every shipment carries the material guarantee: the panel that arrives matches the specification sheet you quoted from. Where a mill certificate or test report applies to your order, it is named on the quotation by holder, number and scope, and released on request.

Terms and paperwork

FCA Cochin suits a sealed container handed to your carrier; FOB Cochin applies where we deliver on board the vessel you nominate; CFR and CIF are quoted where agreed at enquiry. Payment is 50 per cent advance with the order and the balance before dispatch for standard products, and 100 per cent advance for custom specifications, by bank transfer (T/T).
