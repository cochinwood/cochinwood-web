You are not buying us for the distance

Vietnam is a working lane for us rather than an aspiration — one of only two overseas markets named on our own homepage, alongside the UAE. It is also a country with a large wood-products industry of its own. Nobody imports a panel from Kerala because Kerala is close. They import it because a particular construction, at a particular price, in a particular quantity, is easier to get from a mill that will press to your sheet than from whatever is on the local market that week.

So the honest pitch on this lane is narrow and it is about specification. Our group has pressed plywood at Perumbavoor since 1986. We make packing-grade MR board to IS 303, BWR hardwood board, calibrated Okoume-faced panels on a eucalyptus core, and film-faced shuttering ply. You send the thickness, sheet size, bond, face and quantity; we quote FOB Cochin in USD against it, with a loading plan attached. If the number does not beat what you can buy at home, it does not, and you will know within one business day.

One detail that comes up on this lane specifically: Vietnamese consignees, like Gulf ones, tend to flag rough crate surfaces on arrival. Where the cargo will be inspected at destination, an Okoume face on the packing or BWR substrate is worth specifying — lighter, cleaner, and it stencils properly.

Grade choice on a two-week tropical lane

Transit time modified by the climate of the lane is the best predictor of which bond you need. Under ten days in a dry lane, MR or BWR is enough. A voyage of two weeks or more through monsoon Southeast Asia is a different problem: humidity cycling in a steel box is what turns a marginal glue line into a delaminated corner, and the cost of getting it wrong is never the failed crate — it is the cargo claim, the port-side rework and the demurrage while a replacement is built. Our note on BWR vs BWP for export packing sets out where each grade stops working.

Tell us the voyage the cargo faces after it leaves you, not just the one to Vietnam, and we will quote the bond that survives it.

Terms and the first order

Export quotes are in US dollars under Incoterms 2020, and always name the place the term applies to. FCA Cochin suits a sealed container handed to your carrier; FOB Cochin applies where we deliver on board the vessel you nominate. CFR and CIF are quoted where agreed at enquiry. Payment is 50 per cent advance with the order and the balance before dispatch for standard products, and 100 per cent advance for custom specifications, by bank transfer (T/T).

Pre-shipment inspection is welcome, by you or any agency you appoint, and we hold the goods for it. You get photographs and video of your container as it loads, and a packing list carrying the same weights we declare to the shipping line. If you have not traded with us before, every registration the selling entity holds is listed with its government portal on verify us before you pay.
