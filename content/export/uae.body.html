Why the Kochi to Jebel Ali lane works

Our facilities are in Perumbavoor, Kerala, about 40 km from Cochin Port. Containers stuff at the factory, move to the Vallarpadam terminal, and load onto the India–Gulf services calling Jebel Ali. Direct sailings typically take 4 to 7 days port to port; transhipment routings run longer. For a UAE buyer, that means a replenishment cycle measured in weeks, not months, from confirmed order to cargo on the quay.

Jebel Ali clears cargo for Dubai and, by road, the rest of the Emirates, so most of our UAE consignments route through it. If you take delivery at another UAE port, name it in the enquiry and we will quote that routing. The Gulf is one of several lanes we run out of Cochin; the full list is on our export page.

Commercial terms that hold up in procurement

All export quotes are issued in USD. FOB Cochin is the default; CIF Jebel Ali is available on request, priced against current freight. We do not publish rates. Each quote is built against your thickness, size, grade and quantity, and the reply goes out within one business day.

Payment for standard products is 50 percent advance with the balance before dispatch. Custom specifications are 100 percent advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T). Every shipment carries our material guarantee: the panel that arrives matches the spec sheet quoted.

Paperwork, ISPM-15 and loading

Plywood panels are processed wood and exempt from ISPM-15, which removes one layer of phytosanitary paperwork for UAE importers. Assembled boxes, crates and pallets from our line are made under an ISPM-15 compliant process. Commercial invoice, packing list and shipping documents are ready when the container leaves, and loading is planned to the container's weight limit so dense ply is not undercounted or overloaded.
