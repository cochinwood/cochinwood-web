A lane that is bought on paper first

Our group has pressed plywood at Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986, about 40 km from Cochin Port. A buyer in Turkey orders from a specification sheet, an emailed photograph and a bank transfer, long before anyone sees a panel. So the things that decide whether this lane works are unglamorous: that the panel matches the sheet you approved, that the paperwork is ready before the vessel sails, and that you can check who we are without asking us for anything.

All three are answerable. Every shipment carries our material guarantee — same thickness, same bond, same construction as the proforma invoice states. The document set is emailed on sailing, not chased afterwards. And the registrations that prove the selling entity is real sit on one page, each with the government portal you can check it on: see verify us before you pay.

We quote FOB Cochin in USD. You or your forwarder book the ocean carriage and name the discharge port, which keeps our number a clean panel price you can set line by line against any other origin.

Import essentials for Turkey

What your clearing agent needs to land a plywood shipment in Turkey. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; customs duty and any import tax are added at import, on your side of the transaction, not ours.

ItemDetail
HS codeHeading 4412. The national item within 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 turns on panel construction and individual ply thickness as well as face species; finished cases, pallets and drums are generally considered under 4415. Your broker confirms the line, and we state the code we ship under on the commercial invoice.
Customs duty and import taxNot quoted by us and not in our price. Your clearing agent confirms the tariff line and the rate in force. Import tax is not charged on CIF alone: the base is the customs value plus customs duty, and local law may add excise, fees and other incidental charges. Turkey applies a base customs duty of 7% on CIF value for most plywood constructions under HS 4412.31–.39, plus a further 20% additional customs duty under Decision No. 3351 — a combined burden of roughly 27% on CIF, since India does not currently qualify for any FTA or GSP reduction with Turkey. VAT (KDV) is 20%, charged on CIF value plus both duties.

Terms, and what you see before the container sails

Export quotes are in US dollars under Incoterms 2020, and always name the place the term applies to. FCA Cochin is normally right for a sealed container handed to your carrier; FOB Cochin applies where we deliver on board the vessel you nominate. Many letters of credit still call for FOB, and we quote it on request. CFR and CIF are quoted where that is agreed at enquiry.

Payment is 50 per cent advance with the order and the balance before dispatch for standard products, and 100 per cent advance for custom specifications. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T), and the beneficiary name always matches the legal seller named on the proforma invoice.

Before the container leaves: pre-shipment inspection is welcome, by you or any third-party agency you appoint, and we hold the goods for it. You also get photographs and video of your own container as it loads, and a packing list with the same net and gross weights we declare to the shipping line. The full sequence is set out on how we handle an export order.
