The lane: Kochi to Mombasa

Our group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factory sits about 40 km from Cochin Port; containers are stuffed at the factory under our own supervision and gate in at the Vallarpadam terminal, so the inland leg is short and the sheet count in the box is the sheet count on the packing list.

Mombasa is the discharge port on the traffic we quote for Kenya. If you clear somewhere else, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing.

We do not publish sailing schedules. Port-to-port time depends on the carrier and the service, and a number printed on a page is wrong the first time the schedule changes. The sailing is confirmed alongside the quote, and the shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

What matters more than the sailing on this lane is what happens after the box is landed. A container that has to travel inland from the coast is governed by the road weight limit on that leg, not by how much the ship could carry, and plywood is the cargo that finds the limit first. Tell us the limit that applies to your haul and it goes into the loading plan before the container is stuffed. Kenya is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers the rest.

The weighbridge, not the ship, sets the sheet count

Plywood is dense cargo and weighs out long before it cubes out. A container reaches its permitted payload with space still showing, so "how many sheets fit" is a weight calculation: thickness, panel density, pallet weight, and the road limit at the discharge end. Get that last figure wrong and the cost is not theoretical — it is a box stripped at a weighbridge, part-loaded onward, and a delivery date gone.

Every quote we issue carries a loading plan: sheets per container, net and gross weight, loose-loaded and palletised counts where the difference matters. We work both box sizes; a 20 ft is often the right answer on a weight-restricted haul even though a 40 ft looks cheaper per sheet on paper. The arithmetic is set out in our note on 20 ft loading by thickness, and rubberwood plywood container weight covers panel density.

USD quotes on FOB Cochin terms

Export quotes are issued in US dollars, FOB Cochin by default. FCA at a named place is often the better term for a sealed container and we quote it on request. Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom builds are 100% advance. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T).

We do not publish rate lists. Veneer and timber costs move and a printed number goes stale. Send the spec — thickness, sheet size, grade, face, quantity and discharge port — and a written quote comes back within one business day. What FOB Cochin does and does not include is set out in our note on the term, and the whole order path, from spec confirmation to the document set on sailing, is on how we export.

We ask for money in advance, so we make ourselves easy to check. Every registration we hold is listed with the government portal you can verify it on at company verification — the IEC, the FIEO RCMC, the LEI, the CIN and the GSTIN, all against the same legal entity that will invoice you.

ISPM-15, and what certification actually costs you if you ask late

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, pressed under heat and adhesive, so the panels ship without a heat-treatment stamp and without a fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Product certification is a different question, and it is settled before the panels are pressed rather than after. If your import into Kenya needs a particular certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection, say so at enquiry: it decides which mill your order is placed with and it cannot be added once the goods exist. Each mill holds its own BIS licence, so there is no single number covering everything we sell; any certificate that applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Pre-shipment inspection by you or by any third-party agency you appoint is welcome, and we hold the goods for it.
