The lane: Kochi to Port Louis

Our group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factory sits about 40 km from Cochin Port; containers are stuffed at the factory under our own supervision and gate in at the Vallarpadam terminal, so the inland leg is short and the sheet count in the box is the sheet count on the packing list.

Port Louis is the discharge port on the traffic we quote for Mauritius. If you clear somewhere else, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing.

We do not publish sailing schedules. Port-to-port time depends on the carrier and the service, and a number printed on a page is wrong the first time the schedule changes. The sailing is confirmed alongside the quote, and the shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

Mauritius is a small market and we quote it as one. A single 20 ft container, mixed across two or three grades, is a normal order here rather than an awkward one, and no minimum is imposed beyond a full box. That matters more than it sounds: an importer who does not have to buy a 40 ft of one thickness to get a price can carry a wider range on the same working capital. Mauritius is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers the rest.

What Mauritian buyers order

An island market pulls the range towards moisture. Marine plywood to IS 710 in a BWP bond is the panel for boat work, coastal construction and wet areas; commercial and BWR plywood covers furniture, shopfitting and joinery; packing grade and film-faced shuttering cover crating and formwork.

One box, loaded to the limit that applies at your end

Plywood is dense cargo and weighs out before it cubes out, which is why a 20 ft is frequently the right box rather than the small one. A container reaches its permitted payload with space still showing, so the sheet count turns on thickness, panel density, pallet weight and the road limit at the discharge end. Send us the limit that applies on your side and the loading plan is built to it — sheets per container, net and gross weight, loose-loaded and palletised counts. It is on every quote, and our note on 20 ft loading by thickness shows the working.

USD quotes on FOB Cochin terms

Export quotes are issued in US dollars, FOB Cochin by default. FCA at a named place is often the better term for a sealed container and we quote it on request. Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom builds are 100% advance. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T).

We do not publish rate lists. Veneer and timber costs move and a printed number goes stale. Send the spec — thickness, sheet size, grade, face, quantity and discharge port — and a written quote comes back within one business day. What FOB Cochin does and does not include is set out in our note on the term, and the whole order path, from spec confirmation to the document set on sailing, is on how we export.

We ask for money in advance, so we make ourselves easy to check. Every registration we hold is listed with the government portal you can verify it on at company verification — the IEC, the FIEO RCMC, the LEI, the CIN and the GSTIN, all against the same legal entity that will invoice you.

ISPM-15, and what certification actually costs you if you ask late

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, pressed under heat and adhesive, so the panels ship without a heat-treatment stamp and without a fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Product certification is a different question, and it is settled before the panels are pressed rather than after. If your import into Mauritius needs a particular certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection, say so at enquiry: it decides which mill your order is placed with and it cannot be added once the goods exist. Each mill holds its own BIS licence, so there is no single number covering everything we sell; any certificate that applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Pre-shipment inspection by you or by any third-party agency you appoint is welcome, and we hold the goods for it.
