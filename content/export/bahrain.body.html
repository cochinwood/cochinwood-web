Why Bahrain importers buy plywood from India

Bahrain's industrial base — aluminium smelting and downstream fabrication, ship repair, oil and gas services, and a steady construction market — consumes plywood it does not produce locally. Nearly all of it arrives by sea, and India's south-west coast is one of the closer origins for it.

Panels are pressed to your specification around Perumbavoor, Kerala — the plywood cluster where our group has been making plywood since 1986 — and containers load a short haul from Cochin Port. You deal directly with the people who control the glue line, core composition, and thickness tolerance, which matters when a claim or a spec change comes up mid-contract.

Routing: Kochi to Khalifa Bin Salman Port

Containers dispatch from Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. For Bahrain, cargo is routed to Khalifa Bin Salman Port at Hidd, the kingdom's container gateway — from there it is a short road leg to the Sitra, Salmabad, and Hidd industrial areas or into Manama.

We sell FOB Cochin: you or your forwarder book the ocean freight, and we handle export clearance, supervised container stuffing at the factory, and the document set — commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and bill of lading. If your clearing agent needs anything specific, tell us at enquiry stage and it goes into the file from day one.

Plywood is dense cargo. Loads usually reach the container's permitted payload before they fill its volume, so we plan the sheet count against the weight limit rather than promising a full cube — your quotation states exactly how many sheets the box carries.

Some Bahrain importers also serve customers in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province across the King Fahd Causeway. If re-export is part of your plan, say so when you enquire and we pack and mark accordingly. Bahrain is one of several GCC lanes we run — the export hub covers the rest.

ISPM-15, without the confusion

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, and the manufacturing process itself deals with the pest risk the standard targets — so panels ship to Bahrain without heat-treatment stamps, and no fumigation certificate is needed for the plywood itself.

Solid-wood packing is a different matter. Our boxes, crates, and pallets are produced under an ISPM-15 compliant process, so cargo packed in them travels onward without phytosanitary trouble. If you want the background on treatment methods, read our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide.
