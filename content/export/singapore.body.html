Eight to twelve days, and a lot of cargo that keeps going

Cochin to Singapore is a short lane by the standards of ours — 8 to 12 days port to port for a full container load, against 22 to 28 for Hamburg and 26 to 32 for the US West Coast. It is also a lane where a large share of what arrives does not stop. Cargo packed in Singapore goes on again, and the panel that only had to survive twelve days is suddenly being asked to survive the leg after that too.

That is the specification conversation worth having before you order, and it is the one thing about this lane we would want a buyer to read. Nobody imports from Kerala because Kerala is close to Singapore; the region is full of supply. They import because a particular bond, face and thickness is easier to get from a mill that presses to a sheet. Our group has done that at Perumbavoor since 1986, 40 km from Cochin Port.

We quote FOB Cochin in USD. You or your forwarder book the ocean carriage; we manufacture, pack, complete Indian export clearance and deliver on board.

Bond choice: the second voyage is the one that decides

The best predictor of grade requirement is transit time modified by the climate of the lane. Singapore is hot, humid and tropical, and 8 to 12 days out of Cochin puts BWR at the baseline for general cargo. Where the cargo is trans-shipping onward, plan around BWP for the full duration instead — our packing operators’ rule of thumb is that under ten days in a dry lane BWR is enough, and over twenty days, or anything routed through monsoon Southeast Asia, calls for BWP unless the cargo itself is low-value and replaceable.

The cost of getting this wrong is not the failed crate. It is the cargo claim, the port-side rework, the demurrage while a replacement crate is built, and a buyer relationship that gets harder to rebuild. BWR vs BWP for export packing sets out where each bond stops working and what the upgrade actually costs per container.

Tell us the whole journey the cargo faces, not just the leg to Singapore, and we will quote the bond that survives it.

Terms and the first order

Export quotes are in US dollars under Incoterms 2020, and always name the place the term applies to. FCA Cochin suits a sealed container handed to your carrier; FOB Cochin applies where we deliver on board the vessel you nominate; CFR and CIF are quoted where agreed at enquiry. Payment is 50 per cent advance with the order and the balance before dispatch for standard products, and 100 per cent advance for custom specifications, by bank transfer (T/T).
