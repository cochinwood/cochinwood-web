A supply lane built around Cochin Port

Our group has manufactured plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factories sit about 40 km from Cochin Port, so containers move from press to terminal without a long inland leg. Qatar is one of six GCC markets we ship to, alongside the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain; the full list of destinations is on our export hub.

For a Doha importer, the practical result is a short, repeatable pipeline: specification agreed in writing, production against that specification, and a loaded container handed to your carrier at Kochi. The same panel, the same grade, the same dimensions, container after container.

What Qatar buyers order: construction and packing

Orders from Qatar fall into two groups. Construction buyers take film-faced shuttering plywood for concrete formwork, marine plywood (IS 710 BWP) for wet areas, and commercial or BWR panels for fit-out work. Packing and logistics buyers take packing-grade MR plywood for crates and cases, plus ready-made plywood boxes, pallets and cable drums.

Okoume is available as a face veneer on both the MR-packing and BWR grades where a lighter, cleaner-faced panel is wanted. We also make calibrated E1 Okoume-faced panels on a eucalyptus core, 16 mm at roughly 32 kg per sheet, supplied bare or HPL-laminated.

Routing: Kochi to Hamad Port

Hamad Port, south of Doha, is Qatar's main container gateway and the port we route every Qatar shipment to. On FOB Cochin terms your forwarder or carrier books the ocean leg; we produce, pack, load and deliver the container to the terminal at Kochi with the export documentation in order.

Plywood is dense cargo and usually weighs out before it cubes out, so we plan each container to the applicable weight limit and issue a packing list showing sheet count, net and gross weight before dispatch. If you are consolidating panels with plywood boxes and crates or timber runners, we load mixed containers against a single packing list.

ISPM-15: what applies and what does not

A point that saves Qatar importers paperwork: plywood panels themselves are exempt from ISPM-15, because plywood is processed wood and the manufacturing process removes the pest risk the standard targets. Treatment obligations apply to solid wood, meaning pallet runners, crate skids and sawn timber, not to the panels.

Our boxes and crates are produced under an ISPM-15-compliant process, so the solid-wood components arrive treated and marked. The distinction is explained in our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide.
