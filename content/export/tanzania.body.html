The lane: Kochi to Dar es Salaam

Our group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factory sits about 40 km from Cochin Port; containers are stuffed at the factory under our own supervision and gate in at the Vallarpadam terminal, so the inland leg is short and the sheet count in the box is the sheet count on the packing list.

Dar es Salaam is the discharge port on the traffic we quote for Tanzania. If you clear somewhere else, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing.

We do not publish sailing schedules. Port-to-port time depends on the carrier and the service, and a number printed on a page is wrong the first time the schedule changes. The sailing is confirmed alongside the quote, and the shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

Most buyers on a new lane start with one container rather than a programme, and that is a sensible way to begin. A trial load can be mixed — two or three products in one box, each with its own sheet count on the packing list — so you can put more than one grade in front of your own customers before committing to a run. Say what you want to test and the quote is built around it. Tanzania is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers the rest.

Loading: plywood weighs out before it cubes out

Plywood is dense cargo. A container reaches its permitted payload well before the space is full, so the sheet count is a weight calculation — thickness, panel density, pallet weight and the road limit at the discharge end. Send us the road weight limit that applies on your side and the loading plan is built to it. Every quote carries that plan: sheets per container, net and gross weight, loose-loaded and palletised where the difference matters. Our note on 20 ft loading by thickness works through it.

USD quotes on FOB Cochin terms

Export quotes are issued in US dollars, FOB Cochin by default. FCA at a named place is often the better term for a sealed container and we quote it on request. Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom builds are 100% advance. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T).

We do not publish rate lists. Veneer and timber costs move and a printed number goes stale. Send the spec — thickness, sheet size, grade, face, quantity and discharge port — and a written quote comes back within one business day. What FOB Cochin does and does not include is set out in our note on the term, and the whole order path, from spec confirmation to the document set on sailing, is on how we export.

We ask for money in advance, so we make ourselves easy to check. Every registration we hold is listed with the government portal you can verify it on at company verification — the IEC, the FIEO RCMC, the LEI, the CIN and the GSTIN, all against the same legal entity that will invoice you.

ISPM-15, and what certification actually costs you if you ask late

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, pressed under heat and adhesive, so the panels ship without a heat-treatment stamp and without a fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Product certification is a different question, and it is settled before the panels are pressed rather than after. If your import into Tanzania needs a particular certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection, say so at enquiry: it decides which mill your order is placed with and it cannot be added once the goods exist. Each mill holds its own BIS licence, so there is no single number covering everything we sell; any certificate that applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Pre-shipment inspection by you or by any third-party agency you appoint is welcome, and we hold the goods for it.

Import essentials for Tanzania

What your clearing agent needs to land a plywood shipment in Tanzania. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; customs duty and any import taxes are added at import, on the valuation basis your destination's customs law uses.

ItemDetail
HS code4412 (plywood; the national item within 4412.31 / 4412.33 / 4412.34 / 4412.39 turns on panel construction and individual ply thickness as well as face species, and finished cases, pallets and drums are generally considered under 4415 — your broker confirms the line)
Customs duty and import taxesTanzania currently overrides the EAC Common External Tariff on this heading with an annually renewed national measure: instead of the CET headline rate of 25% for HS 44.12 (plywood, veneered panels and similar laminated wood), the EAC Gazette in force (Vol. AT 1 No. 16, 30 June 2026) sets Tanzania’s duty at 35% of CIF value for the year from 1 July 2026 — a stay of application the country has renewed on this heading for at least two consecutive fiscal years running, and due for reconfirmation at the next 1 July cycle. On top of duty, Mainland Tanzania VAT is 18% (charged on CIF plus duty), plus a Railway Development Levy of 2% of CIF value and a Destination Inspection Fee of 0.6% of FOB value. Exact HS sub-line classification and the rate in force on the ship date are still for your clearing agent to confirm.
Landed-cost noteIndicative only — your broker must confirm the destination tariff line and the rates in force. Import VAT is not charged on CIF alone: the base is the customs value plus customs duty, and local law may add excise, fees and other incidental charges.
Conformity, standards and inspectionPlywood (HS 4412, every 8-digit line) sits on the Tanzania Bureau of Standards’ PVoC-regulated goods list, so a Certificate of Conformity from a TBS-appointed agency — currently SGS, Intertek, TÜV Rheinland, WSCT or CCIC — is required before the shipment sails, submitted through TBS’s Online Application System (oas.tbs.go.tz) and authenticated against the shipping documents ahead of customs clearance. The standard per-shipment route charges an inspection fee of about 0.5% of FOB value, subject to a USD 250 minimum. A shipment that arrives without a valid pre-shipment certificate is instead diverted to TBS destination inspection at the port, with the delay and cost that implies. As with every market, tell us at enquiry which certificate or test report your import requires, since it decides which mill the order is placed with and cannot be added once the panels are pressed — inspection by you or an agency you appoint is welcome, and the goods are held for it.
