Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

The questions buyers, specifiers and exporters most commonly ask — about grades, MOQs, lead-times, certifications and shipments.

What is the difference between BWR and BWP plywood?

BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) plywood is bonded with melamine-fortified urea-formaldehyde or phenolic resin as confirmed per order, and is commonly specified against IS 303 — the usual choice for general furniture, panelling and dry interior joinery.

BWP (Boiling Water Proof) plywood is bonded with phenol-formaldehyde resin, conforms to IS 710, and survives prolonged exposure to boiling water. Specify BWP for kitchens, bathrooms, exterior joinery, marine applications and any humid environment.

What thicknesses do you stock?

Standard plywood is manufactured in 4, 6, 9, 12, 16, 19 and 25 mm thicknesses. Block boards run 18, 22, 25, 30, 35 and 40 mm. Container flooring ships at 28 mm. Shuttering plywood is most commonly produced at 12 and 18 mm.

Custom thicknesses and dimensions are available by arrangement — talk to our technical desk.

Do you supply ISI / BIS-marked plywood?

Our plywood is produced under the BIS licence of the mill that makes it — several approved mills, each with its own licence, and the applicable CM/L named on your quotation: IS 710 for BWP marine grade and IS 303 for BWR commercial grade. Block board and flush doors are quoted against IS 1659 or IS 2202 where a buyer specifies them. ISI (BIS) marking is supplied only where a valid licence covers that exact product and grade; the licence holder, number and scope are stated on the quotation. Where no licence applies, no marking or conformity claim is made.

What is your minimum order quantity?

We work at bulk and container scale — typically by the 20'/40' container load. A full load may be mixed across thicknesses, grades and product lines. Part-loads within India and LCL consolidation for export are available where the route and quantity allow; the practical minimum for your destination is confirmed on the quotation.

What are your lead-times?

Catalogue items in stock thicknesses load on the schedule we confirm at quotation. Made-to-order runs (custom thickness, surface treatment or large quantities) are scheduled against the order and confirmed on the PI. Export containers, including ISPM-15 treatment and documentation, load on the date set when the PO is confirmed.

Do you provide samples?

Yes. We send 6″ × 6″ sample swatches by courier free of charge for buyers in India, and at cost for international buyers — quoted with the sample request. Larger physical samples and full-sheet evaluations are available against a quoted fee.

What payment terms do you offer for export buyers?

Standard products transact on 50% advance, balance before dispatch; custom production on 100% advance — by bank transfer (T/T). We do not transact on open account.

Which export markets do you currently ship to?

We supply India domestically and export to Sri Lanka, Turkey, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain, Israel, Vietnam, Bangladesh, Malaysia, Singapore and the Maldives in Asia; South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya, Tanzania and Mauritius in Africa; the Netherlands, Germany and the United Kingdom in Europe; the United States, Haiti, Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic in North America; Chile in South America; and Australia.

Do your packaging products meet ISPM-15?

Plywood and other processed panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Where packaging contains regulated solid-wood components and the destination requires it, those components are treated and IPPC-marked — at our own works or through an approved treatment provider, depending on the order — and the treatment certificate is supplied with the shipment.

Can I visit the operations in Perumbavoor?

Absolutely. We welcome buyers, specifiers and trade partners to walk our group's Perumbavoor operations. Drop us an email at sales@cochinwood.in with your dates and we'll arrange a pickup from Cochin International Airport.

Do you guarantee the material?

We back every order with a material guarantee: the grade, construction and face you ordered are exactly what gets loaded, checked before dispatch. Every consignment ships with its technical data sheet, and we can share feedback from buyers running the same material in service.

How do I get a price?

The fastest path: visit our Contact page, or write to sales@cochinwood.in with product, thickness, quantity and destination port. Our export desk replies within one business day.
