The panel Israeli buyers ask us for

The core of our Israel trade is the calibrated Okoume-face panel: an E1 emission-class board with an Okoume face on a eucalyptus core, sanded and calibrated to a consistent thickness across the sheet. A 16 mm panel weighs about 32 kg, light enough to keep handling and freight sensible, stiff enough for furniture and joinery work. We supply it bare or HPL-laminated.

Okoume is a face veneer, not a grade. The same face goes onto our MR packing plywood (IS 303) and onto BWR boards where the job needs better moisture resistance. If your customers know Okoume from other origins, our species profile covers what the timber is and how it behaves. Full specifications sit on the Okoume plywood page.

Our Israel terms: FOB Cochin, in USD

Every quote we issue for Israel is FOB Cochin. We do not quote CIF Ashdod or CIF Haifa. You, or your forwarder, book the ocean freight and marine insurance; we manufacture, pack, complete Indian export clearance and deliver the goods on board your nominated vessel at Cochin Port. The reasoning is practical. Israeli importers almost always hold their own freight arrangements on the India lane and read its current conditions better than a mill in Kerala can. FOB keeps our number a clean panel price you can compare line by line against any other origin.

ISPM-15, packing and container loading

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. As engineered wood they need no heat-treatment stamp and no fumigation certificate, which removes one document, one cost and one common hold-up at the port of discharge. Where a shipment does include solid wood, pallets or crating, that material goes through an ISPM-15-compliant process. If your customs broker raises it, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide explains the distinction.

Panels are palletised and strapped for sea transit. Dense plywood weighs out before it cubes out, so we plan each 40 ft container against the road weight limit at your end, not just the container's own rating, and confirm the exact sheet count with the quote.

FAQ

Do you quote CIF Ashdod or CIF Haifa?

No. All quotes for Israel are FOB Cochin, in USD. You or your forwarder arrange ocean freight and insurance; we deliver export-cleared goods on board your nominated vessel at Cochin Port.

Does the plywood need ISPM-15 treatment or fumigation?

No. Plywood is engineered wood and is exempt from ISPM-15, so the panels need no heat-treatment stamp or fumigation certificate. Any solid-wood pallets or crating in the shipment follow an ISPM-15-compliant process.

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. Samples are available and chargeable. Most buyers check the Okoume face, thickness calibration and core before committing to a trial container.

What are the payment terms for Israeli buyers?

50% advance with the balance before dispatch for standard products, and 100% advance for custom production. We accept bank transfer (T/T).

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Israel?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Israel; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Ashdod or Haifa. You buy factory-direct from India.
