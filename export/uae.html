Why the Kochi to Jebel Ali lane works

Our facilities are in Perumbavoor, Kerala, about 40 km from Cochin Port. Containers stuff at the factory, move to the Vallarpadam terminal, and load onto the India–Gulf services calling Jebel Ali. Direct sailings typically take 4 to 7 days port to port; transhipment routings run longer. For a UAE buyer, that means a replenishment cycle measured in weeks, not months, from confirmed order to cargo on the quay.

Jebel Ali clears cargo for Dubai and, by road, the rest of the Emirates, so most of our UAE consignments route through it. If you take delivery at another UAE port, name it in the enquiry and we will quote that routing. The Gulf is one of several lanes we run out of Cochin; the full list is on our export page.

Commercial terms that hold up in procurement

All export quotes are issued in USD. FOB Cochin is the default; CIF Jebel Ali is available on request, priced against current freight. We do not publish rates. Each quote is built against your thickness, size, grade and quantity, and the reply goes out within one business day.

Payment for standard products is 50 percent advance with the balance before dispatch. Custom specifications are 100 percent advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T). Every shipment carries our material guarantee: the panel that arrives matches the spec sheet quoted.

Paperwork, ISPM-15 and loading

Plywood panels are processed wood and exempt from ISPM-15, which removes one layer of phytosanitary paperwork for UAE importers. Assembled boxes, crates and pallets from our line are made under an ISPM-15 compliant process. Commercial invoice, packing list and shipping documents are ready when the container leaves, and loading is planned to the container's weight limit so dense ply is not undercounted or overloaded.

FAQ

How long does shipping take from Kochi to Jebel Ali?

Direct sailings from Cochin Port to Jebel Ali typically take 4 to 7 days port to port. Transhipment routings take longer. Add production and stuffing time at our end; we confirm a dispatch schedule with every order.

Do you quote FOB or CIF for UAE orders?

FOB Cochin is our default for export. CIF Jebel Ali is available on request, priced against current freight. All export quotes are issued in USD, and we reply to enquiries within one business day.

Does plywood need ISPM-15 treatment to enter the UAE?

Plywood panels are processed wood and exempt from ISPM-15. Where you buy assembled boxes, crates or pallets, ours are made under an ISPM-15 compliant process.

What are your payment terms for UAE buyers?

Standard products ship on 50 percent advance with the balance before dispatch. Custom specifications are 100 percent advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T).

What HS code is plywood under for the UAE imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.

How much import duty applies to plywood in the UAE?

The GCC Common External Tariff is 5% customs duty on the CIF value. 5% VAT is charged at import on the CIF value. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; duty and VAT are added by your agent at clearance.

What standards or conformity approval does the UAE require?

Clearance references ESMA / ECAS where the product is regulated; GSO standards otherwise. We supply the certificate of origin and product documentation your agent needs; regulated-product certificates are arranged where they apply.

What documents do you provide for the UAE customs?

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and bill of lading — issued so your shipment clears at Jebel Ali or Khalifa Port without hold-ups.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in UAE?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in the UAE; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Jebel Ali. You buy factory-direct from India.
