Two things settle a US order before the price does

Our group has made plywood at Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986, in factories about 40 km from Cochin Port. Selling into the United States is not harder than any other lane we run, but two questions have to be settled at enquiry rather than at the port, and both of them change the quote.

The first is formaldehyde certification. If your entry needs CARB or TSCA Title VI, that decides which mill your order is placed with, and it cannot be added after the panels are pressed. The second is weight. A container of plywood weighs out long before it fills, and the US road figure is the lowest we publish: about 20 tonnes, against a ship-line payload of about 26.7. Both are dealt with below. The export hub covers the rest of the lanes we ship from Cochin.

CARB and TSCA Title VI: name it at enquiry

We make no blanket compliance claim. Each mill holds its own BIS licence and its own test reports, so there is no single licence number that covers everything we sell. Any certificate or test report that applies to your order is identified on the quotation by holder, number and product scope, and released to verified buyers on request. No compliance claim applies to your order until those documents are in your hands.

What that means in practice is short. If your market needs a specific certification — CARB or TSCA Title VI for the United States, FSC chain of custody, a CE Declaration of Performance for an onward European leg — tell us at enquiry. It decides which mill your order is placed with, and it cannot be retrofitted at the port.

A low-emission E1 glue line is available on Okoume-faced panels. E1 and CARB Phase 2 / TSCA Title VI are different regimes with different test methods, so confirm the exact class your entry needs instead of treating one as a substitute for the other. Our note on formaldehyde compliance for plywood exports sets out what to ask any supplier for; the certificate policy in full is on company verification.

Loading to a US road weight, not to the container's rating

Plywood is dense. A 40 ft container loaded to its roughly 26.7-tonne payload with 12 mm sheets fills only about 60% of its 67 cubic metres, so the weighbridge sets the sheet count and the cube never gets a say. Destination road rules cut the number further, and the United States cuts it furthest of the figures we publish: about 20 tonnes for US highway transport.

So we load every container to the lower of the ship-line payload and the destination road limit. Your boxes are not stopped at a weighbridge after arrival because the container was stuffed to a limit that only applies at sea. The figure is approximate and confirmed per booking on the proforma invoice, alongside sheets per container and net and gross weights. The arithmetic behind it — payload caps, sheet weights, worked 40 ft loadings — is on the container weight guide.

Customs dutyThe base US MFN duty on HS 4412 plywood runs Free to 8% depending on the exact 10-digit HTS line, with the general ‘other’ tropical-hardwood plywood lines most CWI shipments fall under — gurjan, marine and similar constructions — typically dutiable at 8%; India receives no GSP or FTA preference on this line, having lost US GSP eligibility in 2019. The India-specific reciprocal tariff that had pushed the combined rate as high as 50% through 2025 no longer applies: the US Supreme Court ruled in February 2026 (Learning Resources v. Trump) that IEEPA does not authorize it, and the underlying executive orders, including the added Russia-oil surcharge, were terminated that same month. Since 24 July 2026, though, a Section 301 ‘forced labor’ action adds a further 10% duty on Indian-origin goods generally, and a separate Section 232 tariff on timber, lumber and derivative wood products is also in force — whether CWI’s specific plywood HTS lines are exempted under either program has not been confirmed from public sources. There is no US anti-dumping or countervailing duty order on plywood from India, only on plywood from China. Because the final rate depends on the exact HTS line and on annex-level exemptions under both currently active programs, your customs broker confirms the current combined rate for the tariff line you are shipping under.

Terms, documents, and checking us before you pay

Export quotes are issued in US dollars under Incoterms 2020, and always name the place or port: FCA Cochin where a sealed container is handed to your carrier, FOB Cochin where we deliver on board, CFR or CIF where agreed. Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom specifications are 100% advance. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T).

Every shipment carries a commercial invoice, a packing list with bundle-wise net and gross weights, the Bill of Lading and a certificate of origin. Tell us your customs broker's checklist when you confirm the order: destination-specific documents are agreed before production ends, not after the container sails.

We ask for money before you have met us, so check us first. IEC AAJCC9689H is searchable at dgft.gov.in; RCMC/FIEO/10262/2024-2025 is our Federation of Indian Export Organisations registration for wood and articles; the LEI 98450010B744B73B1445 is on gleif.org if your bank runs a counterparty check. Each registration and the portal that confirms it is listed on company verification. Pre-shipment inspection is welcome, by you or an agency you appoint, and loading photographs and video of your container go out the same day it stuffs. The whole sequence is on how we handle an export order.

FAQ

Do you supply CARB Phase 2 or TSCA Title VI certified plywood?

We make no blanket claim. Tell us at enquiry that your entry needs CARB or TSCA Title VI: it decides which mill your order is placed with, and it cannot be added after the panels are pressed. Any certificate or test report that applies to your order is identified on the quotation by holder, number and product scope, and released to verified buyers on request.

What HS code do you ship plywood to the United States under?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species); finished cases, pallets and drums are generally considered under 4415. We state the code we ship under on the commercial invoice, and your customs broker confirms the national tariff line and the rate in force.

How many plywood sheets fit in a container for the United States?

Weight decides it, not volume. We load to the lower of the ship-line payload, about 26.7 tonnes, and the destination road limit, which for US highway transport is about 20 tonnes. The exact sheet count and the net and gross weights are confirmed per booking on the proforma invoice.

Do you quote FOB or CIF for US buyers?

FOB Cochin is the default, in USD under Incoterms 2020. FCA Cochin, CFR or CIF are quoted where agreed. Standard products ship on 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom specifications are 100% advance. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T).

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in the United States?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in the United States; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to the US discharge port you nominate. You buy factory-direct from India.
