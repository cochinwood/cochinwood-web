What Qatar buyers order: construction and packing

Orders from Qatar fall into two groups. Construction buyers take film-faced shuttering plywood for concrete formwork, marine plywood (IS 710 BWP) for wet areas, and commercial or BWR panels for fit-out work. Packing and logistics buyers take packing-grade MR plywood for crates and cases, plus ready-made plywood boxes, pallets and cable drums.

Okoume is available as a face veneer on both the MR-packing and BWR grades where a lighter, cleaner-faced panel is wanted. We also make calibrated E1 Okoume-faced panels on a eucalyptus core, 16 mm at roughly 32 kg per sheet, supplied bare or HPL-laminated.

Routing: Kochi to Hamad Port

Hamad Port, south of Doha, is Qatar's main container gateway and the port we route every Qatar shipment to. On FOB Cochin terms your forwarder or carrier books the ocean leg; we produce, pack, load and deliver the container to the terminal at Kochi with the export documentation in order.

Plywood is dense cargo and usually weighs out before it cubes out, so we plan each container to the applicable weight limit and issue a packing list showing sheet count, net and gross weight before dispatch. If you are consolidating panels with plywood boxes and crates or timber runners, we load mixed containers against a single packing list.

ISPM-15: what applies and what does not

A point that saves Qatar importers paperwork: plywood panels themselves are exempt from ISPM-15, because plywood is processed wood and the manufacturing process removes the pest risk the standard targets. Treatment obligations apply to solid wood, meaning pallet runners, crate skids and sawn timber, not to the panels.

Our boxes and crates are produced under an ISPM-15-compliant process, so the solid-wood components arrive treated and marked. The distinction is explained in our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide.

FAQ

How do I import plywood from India to Qatar?

Send the specification: grade, thickness, sheet size and quantity. We reply with a written USD quote on FOB Cochin terms within one business day. On confirmation, standard products ship on 50% advance with the balance before dispatch, and the loaded container is delivered to Cochin Port for carriage to Hamad Port.

Do plywood sheets need ISPM-15 treatment for Qatar?

No. Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15 because plywood is processed wood. Treatment applies to solid timber components such as pallet runners and crate skids, and our plywood boxes and crates are produced under an ISPM-15-compliant process, so those parts arrive treated and marked.

What are the payment terms for Qatar orders?

Standard products are 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom-manufactured items are 100% advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T), and all export quotes are issued in USD.

Which Qatar port do you ship to?

Hamad Port, Qatar's main container gateway south of Doha. We sell FOB Cochin, so your nominated forwarder or carrier books the ocean leg and we deliver the loaded, documented container to the terminal in Kochi.

What HS code is plywood under for Qatar imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.

How much import duty applies to plywood in Qatar?

The GCC Common External Tariff is 5% customs duty on the CIF value. There is no VAT in Qatar. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; duty and VAT are added by your agent at clearance.

What standards or conformity approval does Qatar require?

Clearance references QGOSM standards / QS-Mark where applicable. We supply the certificate of origin and product documentation your agent needs; regulated-product certificates are arranged where they apply.

What documents do you provide for Qatar customs?

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and bill of lading — issued so your shipment clears at Hamad Port without hold-ups.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Qatar?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Qatar; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Hamad Port. You buy factory-direct from India.
