The lane: Kochi to Port Louis

Our group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. Port Louis is the discharge port on the traffic we quote for Mauritius. If you clear somewhere else, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing.

Mauritius is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers the rest.

What Mauritian buyers order

An island market pulls the range towards moisture. Marine plywood to IS 710 in a BWP bond is the panel for boat work, coastal construction and wet areas; commercial and BWR plywood covers furniture, shopfitting and joinery; packing grade and film-faced shuttering cover crating and formwork.

One box, loaded to the limit that applies at your end

Plywood is dense cargo and weighs out before it cubes out, which is why a 20 ft is frequently the right box rather than the small one. A container reaches its permitted payload with space still showing, so the sheet count turns on thickness, panel density, pallet weight and the road limit at the discharge end. Send us the limit that applies on your side and the loading plan is built to it — sheets per container, net and gross weight, loose-loaded and palletised counts. It is on every quote, and our note on 20 ft loading by thickness shows the working.

ISPM-15, and what certification actually costs you if you ask late

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, pressed under heat and adhesive, so the panels ship without a heat-treatment stamp and without a fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Product certification is a different question, and it is settled before the panels are pressed rather than after. If your import into Mauritius needs a particular certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection, say so at enquiry: it decides which mill your order is placed with and it cannot be added once the goods exist. Each mill holds its own BIS licence, so there is no single number covering everything we sell; any certificate that applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Pre-shipment inspection by you or by any third-party agency you appoint is welcome, and we hold the goods for it.

Import essentials for Mauritius

What your clearing agent needs to land a plywood shipment in Mauritius. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; customs duty and any import taxes are added at import, on the valuation basis your destination's customs law uses.

ItemDetail
HS code4412 (plywood; the national item within 4412.31 / 4412.33 / 4412.34 / 4412.39 turns on panel construction and individual ply thickness as well as face species, and finished cases, pallets and drums are generally considered under 4415 — your broker confirms the line)
Customs duty and import taxesMauritius applies a general (MFN) customs duty of 0% on plywood and veneered panels under HS 4412, across every subheading from 4412.10 through 4412.99, per the Mauritius Revenue Authority’s Integrated Tariff Schedule (HS 2022, as at 20 June 2026). Import VAT is 15% — Mauritius’s standard rate — charged on the customs value plus duty, and no excise duty applies to plywood. The general duty is already nil, so a CECPA Certificate of Origin from India adds no further reduction and is not needed for this line; your clearing agent confirms the exact tariff line and the rate in force at the time of import.
Landed-cost noteIndicative only — your broker must confirm the destination tariff line and the rates in force. Import VAT is not charged on CIF alone: the base is the customs value plus customs duty, and local law may add excise, fees and other incidental charges.
Conformity, standards and inspectionNo Mauritius Standards Bureau certification and no import permit is mandated for plywood or wood-based panels imported from India. The Mauritius Revenue Authority’s tariff schedule and its commercial-importers clearance list flag agency sign-off only for specific goods — foodstuffs, meat, fish, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and select consumer products such as toys and cement — and plywood appears against none of them; the same schedule flags phytosanitary (NPPO) clearance for bamboo plywood and wooden picture frames specifically, not for wood-ply panels generally, and import permits are otherwise restricted to a fixed list of controlled goods (wheat flour, petroleum, rice, fireworks, rough diamonds, gold and similar) that does not include any Chapter 44 wood product. Which grade should I order for coastal and wet-area work?

Marine plywood to IS 710 in a BWP bond is the panel built for permanent exposure to moisture; BWR sits below it and MR grade below that. Our note on BWR versus BWP sets out where each one stops. Tell us the application in the enquiry and we will say which bond we would ship, and why.

What import duty will I pay on plywood in Mauritius?

Do the plywood sheets need ISPM-15 treatment or fumigation for Mauritius?

The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply those, they are produced under an ISPM-15 compliant process with the marks and paperwork in order.

What HS code is plywood under for Mauritius imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.
