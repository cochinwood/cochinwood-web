A short lane: Kochi to Sohar

Our factory operates in Perumbavoor, Kerala, about 40 km from Cochin Port, where our group has made plywood since 1986. Containers gate in at the Vallarpadam terminal and cross the Arabian Sea on a direct run. It is one of the shorter export lanes out of India, with transit measured in days rather than weeks, which matters when a site or packing line is waiting on material.

On the Oman side, most container traffic clears through Sohar. Buyers in the Muscat area typically take delivery at Sohar and truck onward. If your operation clears through Salalah instead, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing. Oman sits on the same GCC network we serve into the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, and Bahrain. The export hub covers every lane, and our GCC export notes go deeper on documentation and scheduling.

ISPM-15: what applies and what does not

A point that trips up first-time importers: plywood sheets are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, so panels ship without heat-treatment stamps or fumigation certificates. The rule covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates, or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line and are weighing treatment routes, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Shipping documents are prepared and shared before the vessel sails, so clearance at Sohar can start the day the container lands.

FAQ

How long does a container take from Kochi to Sohar?

It is a direct Arabian Sea run and one of the shorter export lanes from India, with transit measured in days rather than weeks. Exact port-to-port time depends on the carrier and sailing; we confirm the schedule alongside the quote, and shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

Do plywood sheets need ISPM-15 treatment for import into Oman?

No. Plywood is a processed wood product and is exempt from ISPM-15. The rule applies to solid-wood packaging such as pallets and crates; where we supply those, they are produced under an ISPM-15 compliant process with the marks and paperwork in order.

What are your payment terms for Oman orders?

Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance payable before dispatch; custom builds are 100% advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T), and all export invoicing is in US dollars.

How do I get a price for Muscat or Sohar?

Send the thickness, sheet size, grade, quantity, and your discharge port to sales@cochinwood.in or through the quote form. A written USD quote on FOB Cochin terms comes back within one business day.

What HS code is plywood under for Oman imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.

How much import duty applies to plywood in Oman?

The GCC Common External Tariff is 5% customs duty on the CIF value. 5% VAT is charged at import on the CIF value. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; duty and VAT are added by your agent at clearance.

What standards or conformity approval does Oman require?

Clearance references GSO standards via the DGSM. We supply the certificate of origin and product documentation your agent needs; regulated-product certificates are arranged where they apply.

What documents do you provide for Oman customs?

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and bill of lading — issued so your shipment clears at Sohar or Salalah without hold-ups.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Oman?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Oman; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Sohar or Salalah. You buy factory-direct from India.
