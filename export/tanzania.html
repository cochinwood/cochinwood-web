The lane: Kochi to Dar es Salaam

Our group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factory sits about 40 km from Cochin Port; containers are stuffed at the factory under our own supervision and gate in at the Vallarpadam terminal, so the inland leg is short and the sheet count in the box is the sheet count on the packing list.

Dar es Salaam is the discharge port on the traffic we quote for Tanzania. If you clear somewhere else, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing.

We do not publish sailing schedules. Port-to-port time depends on the carrier and the service, and a number printed on a page is wrong the first time the schedule changes. The sailing is confirmed alongside the quote, and the shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

Most buyers on a new lane start with one container rather than a programme, and that is a sensible way to begin. A trial load can be mixed — two or three products in one box, each with its own sheet count on the packing list — so you can put more than one grade in front of your own customers before committing to a run. Say what you want to test and the quote is built around it. Tanzania is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers the rest.

Loading: plywood weighs out before it cubes out

Plywood is dense cargo. A container reaches its permitted payload well before the space is full, so the sheet count is a weight calculation — thickness, panel density, pallet weight and the road limit at the discharge end. Send us the road weight limit that applies on your side and the loading plan is built to it. Every quote carries that plan: sheets per container, net and gross weight, loose-loaded and palletised where the difference matters. Our note on 20 ft loading by thickness works through it.

ISPM-15, and what certification actually costs you if you ask late

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, pressed under heat and adhesive, so the panels ship without a heat-treatment stamp and without a fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Product certification is a different question, and it is settled before the panels are pressed rather than after. If your import into Tanzania needs a particular certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection, say so at enquiry: it decides which mill your order is placed with and it cannot be added once the goods exist. Each mill holds its own BIS licence, so there is no single number covering everything we sell; any certificate that applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Pre-shipment inspection by you or by any third-party agency you appoint is welcome, and we hold the goods for it.

What import duty will I pay on plywood in Tanzania?

Tell us what your import needs at enquiry, not after the goods are pressed. Do the plywood sheets need ISPM-15 treatment or fumigation for Tanzania?

No. Plywood is a processed wood product and is exempt from ISPM-15, so the panels carry no heat-treatment stamp and need no fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply those, they are produced under an ISPM-15 compliant process with the marks and paperwork in order.

What are your payment terms for Tanzania orders?

Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance payable before dispatch; custom builds are 100% advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T), and all export invoicing is in US dollars. If you have not traded with us before, company verification lists every registration we hold and the government portal you can check it on.

What HS code is plywood under for Tanzania imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Tanzania?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Tanzania; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Dar es Salaam. You buy factory-direct from India.
