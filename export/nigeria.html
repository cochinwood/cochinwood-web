The lane: Kochi to Apapa

Our group has made plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factory sits about 40 km from Cochin Port; containers are stuffed at the factory under our own supervision and gate in at the Vallarpadam terminal, so the inland leg is short and the sheet count in the box is the sheet count on the packing list.

West Africa is a different routing from the Gulf and East African lanes, and the container traffic we quote for Nigeria discharges at Apapa in Lagos. If your clearance runs through another terminal, say so in the enquiry and we will quote against that routing.

We do not publish sailing schedules. Port-to-port time depends on the carrier and the service, and a number printed on a page is wrong the first time the schedule changes. The sailing is confirmed alongside the quote, and the shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

The part of a Nigerian import we can actually control is the paperwork leaving our end, and the time to fix it is at enquiry. Tell us what your import requires before the proforma invoice is issued: a document that has to be named on the commercial invoice, a certificate tied to a particular mill, an inspection held before loading. Every one of those is cheap to arrange in week one and expensive to retrofit once a container is on the water. Nigeria is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers the rest.

ISPM-15, and what certification actually costs you if you ask late

Plywood panels are exempt from ISPM-15. Plywood is a processed wood product, pressed under heat and adhesive, so the panels ship without a heat-treatment stamp and without a fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply plywood boxes, crates or pallets, the ISPM-15 process and markings are handled on our side. If you run your own packing line, our note on heat treatment versus methyl bromide sets out the difference.

Product certification is a different question, and it is settled before the panels are pressed rather than after. If your import into Nigeria needs a particular certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection, say so at enquiry: it decides which mill your order is placed with and it cannot be added once the goods exist. Each mill holds its own BIS licence, so there is no single number covering everything we sell; any certificate that applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Pre-shipment inspection by you or by any third-party agency you appoint is welcome, and we hold the goods for it.

Import essentials for Nigeria

What your clearing agent needs to land a plywood shipment in Nigeria. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; customs duty and any import taxes are added at import, on the valuation basis your destination's customs law uses.

ItemDetail
HS code4412 (plywood; the national item within 4412.31 / 4412.33 / 4412.34 / 4412.39 turns on panel construction and individual ply thickness as well as face species, and finished cases, pallets and drums are generally considered under 4415 — your broker confirms the line)
Customs duty and import taxesNigeria applies a 20% import duty on CIF value across every HS 4412 plywood subheading — confirmed directly on the Nigeria Customs Service’s own tariff portal for each line, from bamboo plywood (4412.10) through blockboard and laminboard (4412.51–.59) — placing plywood in Band 3 of the ECOWAS Common External Tariff rather than the top 35% protective band. VAT is 7.5% on imports, charged on CIF value plus duty. India holds no FTA or ECOWAS-origin preference into Nigeria, so the full CET rate applies with no discount. Nigerian customs also layers further charges on top of duty and VAT — an ECOWAS trade levy, a supervision or inspection charge, and a surcharge on the duty amount are all commonly cited — but the rates and bases quoted for these vary between sources, so we do not publish a combined landed-cost figure here; your clearing agent confirms the full charge stack in force at clearance.
Landed-cost noteIndicative only — your broker must confirm the destination tariff line and the rates in force. Import VAT is not charged on CIF alone: the base is the customs value plus customs duty, and local law may add excise, fees and other incidental charges.
Conformity, standards and inspectionNigeria requires SONCAP (Standards Organisation of Nigeria Conformity Assessment Programme) for plywood and other wood panels — they fall outside SON’s narrow exemption list (food, medicines, raw-material chemicals for manufacturers, military equipment, contraband, and most used goods), so the general rule applies. In practice, a Product Certificate is obtained first from SON or an appointed Independent Accredited Firm such as Cotecna or Intertek, based on an accredited test report and valid up to three years, followed by a SONCAP Certificate issued per shipment against the invoice and packing list — this is what your Nigerian importer presents to clear customs, alongside their own Form M and Cargo Tracking Note. Tell us at enquiry so the Product Certificate step can start well before the panels are pressed; inspection by you or an agency you appoint remains welcome and the goods are held for it.

FAQ

Which port do you ship to for Nigeria?

Apapa in Lagos, unless you tell us otherwise. The discharge port is named on the quotation, and if you clear through another terminal we will quote against that routing. We ship full-container loads from Cochin and hold no stock in Nigeria.

How long does a container take from Kochi to Apapa?

It depends on the carrier and the service, and we would rather confirm it for your booking than print a figure the next schedule change makes wrong. The sailing is confirmed alongside the quote, and shipping documents are shared before the vessel sails.

What import duty will I pay on plywood in Nigeria?

We do not state a rate for Nigeria. We have never published one, and a wrong tariff is an expensive thing for a buyer to be handed. Duty and any import tax are set by the destination schedule against the line your goods are classified under, and applied at import. We state the HS code on the commercial invoice and issue the certificate of origin; your clearing agent confirms the line and the rate in force. We do not give customs rulings and will not promise a duty outcome.

Do you supply certification for import into Nigeria?

Tell us what your import needs at enquiry, not after the goods are pressed. A certificate, test report or pre-shipment inspection decides which mill the order is placed with, and it cannot be added retrospectively. Each mill holds its own BIS licence; whatever applies to your order is named on the quotation by holder, number and product scope. Inspection by you or an agency you appoint is welcome and we hold the goods for it.

Can I inspect the goods before they are shipped?

Yes, and it is the sensible thing to do on a first order. Come yourself or appoint any third-party inspection agency; we hold the goods for the inspection window before loading. You also get photographs and video of your container as it is stuffed, sent the same day, and a packing list with real net and gross weights.

Do the plywood sheets need ISPM-15 treatment or fumigation for Nigeria?

No. Plywood is a processed wood product and is exempt from ISPM-15, so the panels carry no heat-treatment stamp and need no fumigation certificate. The standard covers solid-wood packaging — pallets, crates, dunnage. Where we supply those, they are produced under an ISPM-15 compliant process with the marks and paperwork in order.

What are your payment terms for Nigeria orders?

Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance payable before dispatch; custom builds are 100% advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T), and all export invoicing is in US dollars. If you have not traded with us before, company verification lists every registration we hold and the government portal you can check it on.

What HS code is plywood under for Nigeria imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Nigeria?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Nigeria; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Apapa, Lagos. You buy factory-direct from India.
