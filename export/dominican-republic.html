Bond grade decides how the panel arrives

MR is a moisture-resistant bond, not a waterproof one, and weeks of humid air is exactly the condition that finds the difference. Where the panel will sit in damp air after it lands, specify BWR; where it will be wet, IS 710 BWP marine. Our note on BWR versus BWP for export packing sets out where each bond stops. Whichever you take, panels are palletised and strapped for sea transit and each grade ships with its technical data sheet.

Plywood is dense and weighs out before it cubes out: a 40 ft container loaded to its roughly 26.7-tonne ship-line payload with 12 mm sheets fills only about 60% of its 67 cubic metres. We load to the lower of that payload and the road limit at your end, so tell us the limit that applies to your inland leg and the sheet count is set against it on the proforma invoice. The arithmetic is on the container weight guide.

How a first container actually runs

Five steps, and nothing about them is specific to this lane. You send the specification — thickness, sheet size, grade and face, quantity, discharge port. We return a proforma invoice: FOB Cochin in USD, sheets per container, net and gross weights, production lead time and the payment schedule, all in writing. Panels are pressed to the approved spec, and an inspection window is held open before loading, for you or any third-party agency you appoint. The container is stuffed at the factory and gated in at Cochin Port. Documents follow the vessel.

Two things close the gap between your money and the container doors opening: loading photographs and video of your own container, sent the same day it stuffs, and a packing list with real bundle-wise net and gross weights — the same numbers declared to the shipping line. Behind both sits the material guarantee: the panel that arrives matches the specification sheet you quoted from. The full sequence is on how we handle an export order.

Import essentials for the Dominican Republic

What your clearing agent needs to land a plywood shipment. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; duty and any tax at import are added on the destination side.

ItemDetail

Customs dutyThe Dominican Republic applies a base customs duty of 3% on CIF value for plywood under HS 4412, the general (MFN) rate under the country’s CAFTA-DR tariff schedule, since India has no free trade or preferential agreement with the Dominican Republic and does not qualify for any reduced rate. Dominican customs (DGA) also charges a flat 0.4% customs service fee on CIF value, on top of the duty. ITBIS — the Dominican VAT-equivalent under Law No. 253-12 — is 18%, charged on CIF value plus the duty; wood panels do not qualify for the 16% reduced ITBIS band, which is limited to specific foodstuffs. Have your clearing agent confirm the exact 8-digit HS subheading and current rate against DGA’s arancel — the live portal could not be reached directly to verify the rate is unchanged since the 2022 tariff amendment.

Terms, documents, and checking us before you pay

Export quotes are issued in US dollars under Incoterms 2020, and always name the place or port: FCA Cochin where a sealed container is handed to your carrier, FOB Cochin where we deliver on board, CFR or CIF where agreed. Standard products ship against 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom specifications are 100% advance. Payment is by bank transfer (T/T).

We ask for money before you have met us, so check us first. IEC AAJCC9689H is searchable at dgft.gov.in; RCMC/FIEO/10262/2024-2025 is our Federation of Indian Export Organisations registration for wood and articles; the LEI 98450010B744B73B1445 is on gleif.org if your bank runs a counterparty check. Each registration and the portal that confirms it is listed on company verification. The bank beneficiary name always matches the legal seller named on the proforma invoice; if you are ever sent details in which it does not, stop and call the number on this site before remitting anything.

FAQ

Do you quote CIF the Dominican Republic, or only FOB Cochin?

FOB Cochin is the default, in USD under Incoterms 2020. FCA Cochin, CFR or CIF are quoted where agreed, against the freight the routing you choose actually attracts. Standard products ship on 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom specifications are 100% advance, by bank transfer (T/T).

What HS code do you ship plywood to the Dominican Republic under?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species); finished cases, pallets and drums are generally considered under 4415. We state the code we ship under on the commercial invoice, and your clearing agent confirms the national tariff line and the rates in force.

Which bond grade should I order for a humid climate?

MR is moisture-resistant, not waterproof. Where the panel will sit in damp air after it lands, specify BWR; where it will be wet, IS 710 BWP marine. We confirm the bond on the proforma invoice and every grade ships with its technical data sheet.

Can I inspect the goods before the container sails?

Yes. Pre-shipment inspection is welcome, by you or any third-party agency you appoint, and we hold the goods for it before loading. We also send loading photographs and video of your container the same day it stuffs, and a packing list with the bundle-wise net and gross weights declared to the shipping line.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in the Dominican Republic?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in the Dominican Republic; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to the Dominican discharge port you nominate. You buy factory-direct from India.
