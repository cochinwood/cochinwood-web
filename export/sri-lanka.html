The shortest export lane out of India

Our group has been making plywood in Perumbavoor, Kerala since 1986. The factories sit about 40 km from Cochin Port, so a container that finishes loading in the morning can gate in at the terminal the same day. From there, Colombo is a 2-4 day sail. Few export lanes out of India are shorter.

A short lane changes how you can buy. Instead of holding months of stock against a long sail, you can order smaller lots more often, keep less capital tied up in inventory, and recover quickly when a project runs ahead of plan. The gap between paying your balance and receiving material is measured in days, not weeks.

The panel that lands in Colombo matches the spec sheet you approved: same thickness, same bond, same construction, shipment after shipment. Sri Lanka is one of several lanes we serve; the export hub covers our GCC and Israel trade.

What we ship to Sri Lanka

Four product lines cover most of what buyers in Sri Lanka source from India: packing-grade plywood for cases and crating, commercial plywood for furniture and interior work, marine plywood for boats and wet areas, and ready-made plywood boxes and crates for exporters who want packaging delivered, not built.

ISFTA duty concessions are worth a question before you order

The India-Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement (ISFTA) grants duty concessions on a wide range of Indian-origin goods entering Sri Lanka. Whether a particular plywood line qualifies, and at what rate, depends on the HS code, Sri Lanka's tariff schedule at the time of import and the rules-of-origin conditions attached to it.

We do not give customs rulings and we will not promise a duty outcome; your clearing agent in Colombo has the final word. What we do is practical: confirm the HS code we ship each product under, and prepare the certificate of origin and export documents a concession claim needs. Ask us about ISFTA eligibility for your HS code when you request a quote, and put the same question to your agent. Ten minutes of checking can change the landed cost of a container.

Terms, currency and paperwork

Export quotes are issued in USD. FOB Cochin is our default term; we quote CIF Colombo on request with sea freight included, so you can compare landed numbers directly. Standard products ship against 50 percent advance with the balance before dispatch; custom-made items are 100 percent advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T).

Every shipment carries our material guarantee: the panels match the agreed grade, thickness and construction, and each grade ships with its technical data sheet. Documentation is ready when the container sails: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin.

One point that surprises first-time importers: plywood panels are processed wood and exempt from ISPM-15 treatment, so bare panels need no heat-treatment stamp to clear. The boxes and crates we build are made under an ISPM-15 compliant process for onward export use.

FAQ

How long does shipping take from Kochi to Colombo?

Port-to-port transit is typically 2-4 days, which makes Kochi to Colombo one of the shortest export lanes out of India. Add your local clearance and haulage time to plan door-to-door delivery.

Do you quote FOB Cochin or CIF Colombo?

Both. FOB Cochin is the default; if you prefer a single landed figure, we quote CIF Colombo with sea freight included. All export quotes are in USD.

Will my plywood shipment qualify for ISFTA duty concessions?

It depends on the HS code, Sri Lanka's tariff schedule and rules-of-origin conditions, so we do not guarantee a duty outcome. We confirm the HS code we ship under and provide the certificate of origin; your clearing agent confirms eligibility on the Sri Lankan side.

Does plywood from India need ISPM-15 treatment for Sri Lanka?

Plywood panels are processed wood and exempt from ISPM-15, so no heat-treatment marking is required on the panels themselves. Plywood boxes and crates we supply are built under an ISPM-15 compliant process.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Sri Lanka?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Sri Lanka; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Colombo. You buy factory-direct from India.
