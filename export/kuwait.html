What Kuwait importers order from us

Most enquiries from Kuwait fall into two baskets. The first is packing-grade plywood: MR bonded, IS 303, in 6, 8, 12, 15 and 18 mm, bought by crate makers, machinery packers and re-export businesses working around the Shuwaikh and Shuaiba industrial areas. The second is commercial and BWR plywood for joinery, fit-out and site work. Okoume is available as a face veneer on both the MR packing and BWR grades where a buyer wants a lighter, cleaner face.

Beyond sheet material, we supply ready-made plywood boxes and crates, plywood pallets, cable drums, film-faced shuttering plywood for formwork, IS 710 BWP marine plywood and container flooring ply. A mixed container is normal; many Kuwait orders combine packing sheets with a quantity of shuttering or commercial panels.

Routing: Cochin to Shuwaikh and Shuaiba

We quote FOB Cochin as the default. Your forwarder or line books the sea leg; we deliver the container to the terminal at Cochin, complete export customs and hand over a clean document set. Shuwaikh, the container gateway for Kuwait City, and Shuaiba, serving the industrial belt to the south, are both routine discharge ports on our GCC shipments. Nominate whichever suits your clearance agent and final delivery point; the paperwork is identical.

Plywood is dense cargo, so containers weigh out before they cube out. We plan each load sheet by sheet against the applicable weight cap and share the packing list, with net and gross weights and sheet counts per thickness, before the container gates in. No surprises at the weighbridge on either end.

Our operation at Perumbavoor sits about 40 km from Cochin Port, which keeps inland haulage short and cut-off dates dependable.

ISPM-15: panels are exempt, crates are covered

A point that saves Kuwait importers real time: plywood panels themselves are exempt from ISPM-15, because plywood is processed wood. Sheet shipments need no heat-treatment mark and no fumigation certificate at either end. Where you order finished boxes, crates or pallets, we produce them under an ISPM-15 compliant process so the packaging clears without query. The difference between heat treatment and methyl bromide fumigation is explained in our note on ISPM-15 treatment methods.

FAQ

Do plywood panels need ISPM-15 fumigation to clear Kuwait customs?

No. Plywood is processed wood and is exempt from ISPM-15, so sheet shipments need no heat-treatment mark or fumigation certificate. Finished boxes, crates and pallets we supply are produced under an ISPM-15 compliant process.

Should we nominate Shuwaikh or Shuaiba as the discharge port?

Either works. Shuwaikh is the container gateway for Kuwait City; Shuaiba serves the industrial area to the south. Since we ship FOB Cochin, your forwarder books the sea leg to whichever port suits your clearance and delivery plan, and our documents are identical for both.

What are your payment terms for Kuwait orders?

USD invoicing on FOB Cochin terms. Standard products ship on 50% advance with the balance before dispatch; custom specifications are 100% advance. We accept bank transfer (T/T).

Can you supply Okoume-faced plywood to Kuwait?

Yes. Okoume is a face veneer option on our MR packing and BWR grades. We also produce calibrated E1 panels with an Okoume face and eucalyptus core, a 16 mm sheet weighing roughly 32 kg, supplied bare or HPL-laminated.

What HS code is plywood under for Kuwait imports?

Plywood falls under HS heading 4412 (sub-headings 4412.31, 4412.33, 4412.34 and 4412.39 by face species). We state the correct code on the commercial invoice for your clearing agent.

How much import duty applies to plywood in Kuwait?

The GCC Common External Tariff is 5% customs duty on the CIF value. There is no VAT in Kuwait. We quote FCA or FOB Cochin in USD; duty and VAT are added by your agent at clearance.

What standards or conformity approval does Kuwait require?

Clearance references KUCAS (Kuwait Conformity Assurance Scheme) — Technical Evaluation Report for regulated goods. We supply the certificate of origin and product documentation your agent needs; regulated-product certificates are arranged where they apply.

What documents do you provide for Kuwait customs?

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and bill of lading — issued so your shipment clears at Shuwaikh or Shuaiba without hold-ups.

Do you have a plywood factory or warehouse in Kuwait?

No — Cochin Wood is an Indian plywood factory near Cochin (Kochi), Kerala. We do not hold local stock or a warehouse in Kuwait; we ship full-container loads FOB Cochin to Shuwaikh or Shuaiba. You buy factory-direct from India.
