The details make the difference

Your specification.
Through every step.

A clear brief becomes a clear order. Here is how we work through the material, checks and delivery with you.

01 / From enquiry to delivery

Start with the specification.

Tell us the application, grade, dimensions and quantity. We’ll help resolve the details that affect performance and price.

02 / From enquiry to delivery

Agree the material and finish.

Core, surface, tolerances and any special preparation are recorded in the order. The specification guides production.

03 / From enquiry to delivery

Define the checks that matter.

Confirm the inspection points and documents for your order. Samples, moisture checks and packing details can be agreed before dispatch.

04 / From enquiry to delivery

Plan for the destination.

Delivery terms, packing and dispatch documents are agreed around your route. For export, start with the destination port.
