# Cochin Wood website usability test

## Purpose

Check whether an unfamiliar buyer can find a suitable product, understand the
starting specification, prepare a multi-thickness enquiry and finish it on a
phone. This is a research protocol only until real participants complete it;
internal agent or browser checks must not be reported as buyer research.

## Participants

Target 3–5 people who buy plywood, build crates/cases, manage formwork or
arrange export packing. Record role, market and whether they have used
cochinwood.in before. Do not collect customer contact details in the notes.

## Session script (15–20 minutes)

1. Give the participant a short scenario: “You need plywood for your work. Use
   this site as you normally would and tell me what you would ask the supplier.”
2. Ask them to find the product they would start with. Do not name a product or
   open a page for them. Record the first route and any wrong turns.
3. Ask: “What grade, core/face, thickness and limitation do you understand from
   this page?” Record their words, not an interpretation.
4. Ask them to prepare one enquiry containing two different thicknesses. Watch
   whether they discover **Duplicate item** or **Add another product**, whether
   each quantity stays with its own item, and whether the unit is understood.
5. On a phone, ask them to enter a destination, optional Indian PIN if relevant,
   and an optional required-by date. Do not submit a live enquiry. Use the
   isolated intercepted test environment or stop before the final button.
6. Ask what they expected to happen next and what, if anything, felt uncertain.
