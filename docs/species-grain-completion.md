# Species wood photograph sourcing — 6 September 2026

Status: seven of the eleven missing wood-reference photographs have been added to source. The encyclopedia now has **52 unique reference images across 28 species**, including real wood photographs for **24 species**. All 45 earlier image records remain unchanged. This document records source preparation and review, not a production-deployment claim.

The four remaining wood-reference gaps are **Melia dubia, neem, sal and kadam**. Their existing botanical references remain available. No generated wood texture or photograph of an unverified substitute species has been added.

## Processing and provenance

- New source entries live in `content/species-media.json`; assets are `assets/photos/files/Species/{birch,eucalyptus,jackwood,venteak,matti,anjili,poplar}-wood*.webp`.
- Five larger photographs have 320, 640, 960 and final-size variants. Anjili has 320 and 370px variants; Poplar has 320, 640 and 880px variants. No photograph is enlarged during encoding.
- EXIF orientation is applied before resizing. Museum labels and the five original compositions are retained. Anjili's source-panel extraction is separately recorded; no generated detail, grain replacement, retouching or colour styling is applied.
- All 25 new WebPs contain XMP creator, licence and source metadata. The five CC BY-SA derivatives retain CC BY-SA 4.0; Anjili retains CC BY 4.0; Poplar records its public-domain source.
- JSON records preserve source URLs, source-document/picture hashes, derivative hashes, original descriptions, identification basis, licence URLs and transformation notes.
- Each new photograph is assigned only to its species-reference placement. Responsive derivatives are alternatives for that placement, not additional photographs to reuse elsewhere. Do not also place the same photograph in a hero, catalogue tile or another species page.
- Captions distinguish veneer, end-grain cross-section and museum specimen. Anjili uses `max_display_width: 370`; the shared figure renderer supports this cap without truncating its credit.

## Search evidence and acceptance checks

The release artifact folder contains `species-grain-search-expanded.json` (20 original exact-taxon, genus and collection queries) and `species-grain-french-source-search.json` (13 additional French/common-name/alias queries, including all four requested CIRAD/BnF families). The Populus group result was accepted after confirming the guide's genus-wide scope and inspecting its real wood photograph. The other results yielded no accepted wood match for the four unresolved taxa. Direct indexed NumBA and Gallica searches for *Shorea robusta*, *Anthocephalus cadamba*, *Azadirachta indica* and *Populus deltoides* also returned no result. An index miss does not prove the underlying institution lacks a physical specimen.

`species-grain-integration-receipt.json` records the final focused checks: all old image records preserved, all 52 source and main-derivative hashes unique, new variants present with correct dimensions and embedded licences, and the Anjili native-width cap. `species-grain-responsive-additions.json` is the explicit manifest addition supplied to the build owner; the shared responsive manifest is not edited by this sourcing task.

Before publication, the build owner must run the normal preservation and visual-coverage gates and inspect the actual specimen section at mobile and desktop widths, including readable credits and Anjili's 370px cap. Real wood colour varies with cut, ageing, lighting and surface finish; the displayed source photographs are references and do not replace a confirmed physical order sample.
