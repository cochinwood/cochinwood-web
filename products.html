Plywood — six grades

Plywood, every grade we manufacture.

Marine, structural, commercial. Each grade with its own bonding, core build and intended end use. Specify the application — we'll match the grade.

Marine

BWP Grade PF Marine Plywood

Boiling-Water-Proof marine plywood to IS 710, bonded with a BWP-type adhesive conforming to IS 848, with hardwood core veneers. Withstands prolonged submersion — boats, kitchens, exterior joinery.

  • 4–25 mm
  • IS 710 / BWP

MR packing plywood built for crates, cases and pallets — transit-stable, ISPM-15-exempt panels with Okoume or Gurjan face options for export work.

Light, even-grained Okoume face on MR/BWR cores — plus calibrated E1 panels with eucalyptus core, bare or HPL-laminated, for GCC, Israel and Sri Lanka buyers.

MR

Commercial / Packing-Grade Plywood

Moisture-Resistant commercial-grade plywood with urea-formaldehyde bonding. The everyday workhorse for furniture, panelling, partitioning and dry interiors.

BWR

BWR Grade Hardwood Plywood

Boiling-Water-Resistant hardwood plywood bonded with melamine-fortified UF or phenolic resin as confirmed per order, with Okoume or gurjan/keruing face veneers. For kitchens, wardrobes, semi-humid joinery.

High-density film-faced shuttering boards for concrete formwork. Achievable reuse depends on film weight, concrete chemistry, release agent, handling and edge care. Mirror finish on the slab, dimensional stability under load.

Also on file: Lacey Act declarations, FIEO membership, RCMC certificate, and a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI). These are the specifications we ship to, not certificates held in our own name. BIS licences sit with the mill that produces your order — each approved mill holds its own, so no single CM/L number covers everything — while formaldehyde, chain-of-custody and conformity documents are held by the producing unit or its certification body. Which of them apply depends on the mill allocated to your order. Every certificate is a genuine original, identified on the quotation by holder, number and product scope, and released to verified buyers on request. If your market needs a specific certification — CARB or TSCA Title VI for the United States, FSC chain of custody, a CE Declaration of Performance — tell us at enquiry. It decides which mill your order is placed with, and it cannot be added after the panels are pressed. Our hardwood is plantation-grown, supplied with Forest Department NOC.
