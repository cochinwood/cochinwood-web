The industrial picture

A second-tier city that does a lot of physical work

Davangere sits in the dead centre of Karnataka, on NH-48 between Bengaluru and Hubballi, and it is one of those industrial cities that quietly moves a lot of cargo — cotton ginning, spinning and weaving on one side, agri-processing and farm machinery on the other. Both push finished goods out of Davangere on trucks, and a fair share of that cargo travels in wooden boxes, on wooden pallets, or inside containers laid with plywood flooring. That is where a Kochi-based packing-grade plywood supply starts to make economic sense for Davangere buyers.

Historically the cotton-textile capital of Karnataka, Davangere's manufacturing spine runs through the Bapuji Industrial Estate inside city limits, the KSSIDC industrial area at Lokikere Road, the Karur and Anagodu cluster on the Harihar side, and the larger KIADB belts at Harihar and Haralapura. Around the edges, the Davangere–Harihar corridor on NH-48 carries a dense run of cotton mills, ginning units, oil mills, foundries and agri-equipment fabricators.

The plywood demand that flows out of this belt is fairly predictable:

  • Textile machinery and spares — ring frames, autoconers, looms and their replacement parts move in heavy wooden crates, often inter-state, and need square, dense ply that holds nails without splitting.
  • Agri-equipment and pump-set packing — irrigation pumps, sprayer assemblies, threshers and tractor spares are crated for dealer dispatch across South and Central India.
  • Cotton bales and yarn cartons on pallets — finished yarn, processed cotton and ginned lint move on wooden pallets to mills, ports and inland warehouses.
  • Foundry castings and engineered components — the city's long-standing foundry cluster needs heavy-duty box bottoms and skidded crates for iron castings.
  • Export-bound agri-processed goods — areca-nut, maize products and processed grains routed via the Karnataka and Andhra ports, where compliant crating becomes a factor.

The common thread: durable, predictable, fairly thick ply in 6 mm to 18 mm with consistent quality across truckloads. Not retail-counter ply.

What we ship

The Davangere stack, built to spec

We manufacture our packing range in Perumbavoor and supply pan-India out of our own facilities. For Davangere, the typical stack is as follows.

Packing-grade plywood (the workhorse)

Commercial / rubberwood packing-grade ply is what most Davangere crate-makers and pallet shops actually need. It is engineered for nail-holding strength, dimensional stability and a consistent thickness — not for furniture polish. We ship it in standard 8' x 4' sheets across the packing thickness range:

Wooden crates and packing cases

For buyers who would rather receive finished crates than build them in-house, we also supply fabricated boxes — closed, slatted, or skidded — built around the customer's product drawing. Typical Davangere builds: 1.2 m x 0.8 m x 1.0 m machinery crates with 12 mm walls and runner skids, or larger 2.0 m boxes for full ring-frame assemblies.

Pallets

Standard four-way entry pallets in 1200 x 1000 mm and 1200 x 800 mm (Euro), plus custom footprints for cotton-bale stacking, are built from sawn-timber decks or plywood tops with hardwood runners.

Container flooring and base boards

18 mm packing ply in 8' x 4' sheets is the common floor liner for ODC and break-bulk container loads moving from inland Davangere down to Mangaluru or across to Chennai. It protects the container metal floor and gives forklifts a clean working surface.

Okoume face-veneer option

Okoume is a premium export-grade face veneer we offer on the packing and BWR grades. For most Davangere domestic packing it is overkill. It earns its price when the buyer is shipping high-value finished goods — engineered textile machinery for export, precision spares, electronics — where surface finish, light weight and long sea-freight life matter.

Shuttering plywood and sawn timber

Film-faced shuttering ply for the construction trade and rough sawn timber for runners and skids round out the supply.

Logistics

Kochi to Davangere, lane and paperwork

The road distance from Kochi/Ernakulam to Davangere is roughly 580–620 km, depending on whether the truck routes via Salem–Bengaluru on NH-44/NH-48 or via Mysuru on NH-766 and NH-275. The cleaner heavy-truck lane is the Salem–Bengaluru–Tumakuru–Chitradurga–Davangere run on NH-48, the standard six-lane corridor.

  • Transit time: a fully loaded part-load or full truck typically reaches Davangere with transit confirmed at booking from dispatch, weather and check-posts permitting.
  • Truck type: 32-ft single-axle or 22-ft container trucks are the practical sizes; the 32-ft handles a full pallet/crate load comfortably with room for top-stacked ply sheets.
  • Documentation: an e-way bill from Kerala (origin) to Karnataka (destination) is standard; GST is charged as IGST since it is an inter-state movement.
  • Port option: for the rare buyer who would rather receive at a Karnataka port, Mangaluru is the closer one (~250 km from Davangere). Most Davangere buyers find direct road from Kochi simpler and cheaper than the port detour.
  • Export crating: for sea-freight consignments we build to ISPM-15 with heat-treated, IPPC-stamped softwood frames, so the box clears phytosanitary inspection at the port.

Choosing a grade

Which grade for which Davangere load

A practical shortlist we walk every new Davangere buyer through before quoting:

  • Domestic crate for a textile machine or agri pump → commercial packing ply, 12–16 mm walls, 18 mm base. Don't pay for Okoume.
  • Pallet for cotton bales or yarn cartons → 12 mm packing-ply deck on hardwood runners, or full sawn-timber deck. Pick by weight and re-use cycle.
  • Container floor liner for an ODC load to a port → 18 mm packing ply, full 8' x 4' sheets, laid edge-to-edge.
  • Export crate for engineered textile machinery → Okoume face veneer if the customer specifies a clean export look, on an ISPM-15 treated softwood frame with the IPPC stamp.
  • Shuttering for a Davangere construction site → film-faced shuttering ply, separate price list, ordered by the repetition count needed.

When in doubt, share the product dimensions, gross weight, dispatch mode (truck vs container vs port) and monthly off-take. We will revert with a thickness recommendation and a price per sheet or per finished crate.

FAQ

Common questions from Davangere buyers

What is the minimum order to Davangere?

Practical minimum is a part-load of about 8–10 tonnes (roughly 250–300 sheets of mid-thickness packing ply, or a mixed crate-and-pallet consignment). Smaller trial orders are possible, but freight per sheet becomes uneconomical below that range.

Can you cut sheets to crate sizes before dispatch?

Yes. Repeat crate orders are usually cheaper to cut at our end and ship as a kit — the buyer saves on local cutting labour and waste. Share the BOM with grain direction and we will quote both ways.

Do you offer a GST credit invoice and e-way bill?

Every consignment ships with a proforma upfront, a tax invoice on dispatch (IGST for Karnataka), and a Kerala-to-Karnataka e-way bill. No cash, no kachcha bills.
