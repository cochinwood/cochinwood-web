Export-grade plywood for Hyderabad's bulk-drug, vaccine, aerospace and Pharma City exporters.

Hyderabad ships about $5.9 billion in pharma exports a year — roughly 18% of India's total — from 214 USFDA-approved facilities. Telangana supplies about 65% of India's vaccine output. The GMR Aerospace SEZ at Shamshabad hosts multiple OEM joint ventures. And Pharma City at Yadadri — 19,000 acres, the world's largest planned pharma cluster — has 150+ anchors signed for Phase 1. Hyderabad's packaging demand is specialised: cleanroom-suitable, defence-spec, fully traceable. We supply Kochi-origin plywood and crating into that market, 970 km by road.

Why source from Kochi

Packaging supply for Genome Valley, Pharma City and the GMR Aerospace SEZ.

Hyderabad's bulk-drug and formulation exporters — concentrated around Pashamylaram, Patancheru, Jeedimetla and Bonthapally — palletise daily for FDA-audited buyers in the US, EU and Latin America. Vaccine and biotech exporters at Genome Valley need cold-chain validated low-moisture ply. GMR Aerospace SEZ tenants need custom-CAD aerospace crates with internal cradles. The defence cluster at the city's edge needs MIL-spec sealed crates with desiccant cavities — we build the timber case and the desiccant cavity to a drawing, but the MIL specification itself is certified by the buyer's own approved packer, not by us. The Kongara Kalan electronics belt ships light pallets and corner-protected boxes for air cargo. ICD Sanathnagar (the South-Central zone's largest, 3,000-TEU stack, 45-wagon siding) handles stuffing onto rail for Krishnapatnam, Visakhapatnam and Chennai Port. We supply Kochi-origin plywood into that market.

Logistics

Kochi to Hyderabad — 970 km, two days on NH-544 / NH-44.

The route runs Kochi-Salem-Bengaluru-Anantapur-Kurnool-Hyderabad on NH-544 then NH-44 — about 970 km, two-day truck transit for a 32-ft trailer. Loads drop at your facility or route directly to ICD Sanathnagar (CONCOR's South-Central flagship) for stuffing onto rail to Krishnapatnam, Visakhapatnam or Chennai Port. For exporters who consolidate plywood with Kerala hardwood runners for in-house crate assembly, we ship both on one truck. For overseas cargo we supply ISPM-15 heat-treated, IPPC-stamped wood with phytosanitary documentation, and original certificates released to verified buyers on request — with the documents applicable to the destination and agreed order. Share destination port and monthly volume.

Hyderabad FAQ

Common questions from Hyderabad buyers

Does ISPM-15 establish pharmaceutical packaging suitability?

ISPM-15 addresses plant-pest risks in solid-wood packaging; it does not certify pharmaceutical, food-contact or cold-chain suitability. We supply treated and IPPC-marked solid-wood components where required. Packaging made wholly from processed plywood is exempt from ISPM-15. Ask your importer or responsible packer to confirm any separate product-specific requirements before ordering. USDA APHIS explains the wood-packaging scope.

Can you supply low-moisture ply for vaccines or aerospace?

Share the moisture target, handling conditions and packing drawing. We will confirm the achievable specification and agreed inspection evidence in the quote. Any cold-chain, pharmaceutical or aerospace qualification must be agreed with your responsible packer; it is not a certification of our plywood.

Can you deliver to ICD Sanathnagar?

Yes. ICD Sanathnagar (CONCOR's South-Central zone hub, 3,000-TEU stack) is a regular drop. Trucks from Kochi reach in two days; stuffing can be timed to your rail schedule.

Is the wood ISPM-15 compliant for export?

Export pallets and crates are heat-treated and IPPC-stamped to ISPM-15, with phytosanitary documentation for the destination country.

Crating from Pharma City, Genome Valley or the GMR SEZ?

Send spec, moisture target and destination port — we will quote ex-Kochi with delivery to Hyderabad or ICD Sanathnagar.
